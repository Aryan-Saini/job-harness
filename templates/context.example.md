# About me

Everything the models read when a form asks something profile.json doesn't cover: "Why us?",
"Tell us about a project", "How many years with React?". Write facts, not marketing. The more
specific, the better the answers. Nothing here is invented on your behalf, so if it's not
written down, the harness leaves the field for you.

The full guide, with every section forms ask about and examples:
https://github.com/Aryan-Saini/job-harness/blob/main/docs/context.md
