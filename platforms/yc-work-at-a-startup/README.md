# Y Combinator Work at a Startup

`ycombinator.com/companies/<company>/jobs/<id>`, which applies through `workatastartup.com`.

Overnight run: **73 attempts, 10% submitted.** Almost all of the gap is the weekly limit.

## How the form behaves

- You need a Work at a Startup account with a filled profile. Log in once. Never sign up twice.
- Apply opens a box like "Reach out to <founder> at <company>". That message **is** the
  application. Sending it is submitting. There's no separate Submit page.
- Each account can send about **5 applications a week**. After that, Send silently does
  nothing: the dialog just stays open, with no error.

## What works in jh

- The site is recognized and its questions are catalogued.
- A note box is free text, answered by the write model from your `context.md`. Write a
  "Founder note" standard answer there (below).

## Known failures and fixes

| Symptom | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Send does nothing, dialog stays open | The weekly limit is spent | Stop for the week. Apply through the company's own Ashby, Greenhouse or Lever page instead, if it has one |
| Note looks typed but Send does nothing | The note was pasted or set by a script and the app's state is empty | Type the note with real keystrokes |

## The founder note

The note is the whole application, and a founder reads it in seconds. What works:

- 3 to 5 sentences, about 60 to 110 words.
- Open on their product: one specific thing from the posting or the company's one-liner, and
  an honest reaction to it.
- Connect one piece of your real work to their problem. One thing, not a list.
- End with what you'd dig into first, or a real question about their product.
- No stat dumps, no resume recap, no cover-letter phrases ("I'm excited to apply", "I came
  across your posting").
