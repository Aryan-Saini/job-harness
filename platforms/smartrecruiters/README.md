# SmartRecruiters

`jobs.smartrecruiters.com/<company>/<id>`.

Overnight run: **27 attempts, 41% submitted.** Too few to find a pattern in the failures.

## How the form behaves

- Contact details, resume and screening questions.
- Like most modern ATS forms, it can fill some fields from your resume after upload, so check
  them before you submit.

## What works in jh

- Plain fill: text, selects, radios, resume upload.
- The same posting is recognized by its id.

## Known failures and fixes

Nothing specific to SmartRecruiters showed up more than once. The general rules apply: real
clicks and keystrokes. If you hit something that repeats, a PR here is welcome.
